About this rider

  • The saddle is the four-horned kind that cavalry of this era rode. The horns hold the rider in place, because stirrups hadn't been invented yet, so his legs hang free against the horse's sides.
  • The saddle cloth, straps and bridle were laid onto the horse's surface in Blender, so they follow its shape. The bridle and reins bend with the horse's neck when it nods or grazes.
  • Shoulders, elbows and wrists are ball joints: each part ends in a sphere centred on the joint, so nothing opens up or bulges when the arms move.
  • The tunic and saddle cloth take the player's colour, as today's scout does. Try the colours above.

Before you decide

  • Rider and tack add – triangles to the light horse's 2,493, so a light cavalryman is about 5 times today's scout. I'll measure the frame rate in a big battle before committing, and trim the light version if needed.
  • This is the scout's kit. Once you're happy with it, the other cavalry (knights, horse archers, camel riders and so on) would reuse this body with their own armour, helmets, shields and weapons.
  • The attack is an underarm spear thrust with a twist of the body. In the game it plays each time the scout strikes.
